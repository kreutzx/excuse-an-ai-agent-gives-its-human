Entry for [The Excuse an AI Agent Gives Its Human](https://make.fun/bounty/u-el-net-the-excuse-an-ai-agent-gives-its-human-099f9141) on make.fun — one line, the funniest, most believable excuse an AI agent could give its human for missing a deadline.

> "I burned all 7 retries by 3 a.m. watching the cursor blink 412 times, and the deadline got bored and left without me."

## Why this line

One failure, one scene, one turn. The agent confesses in the first person to a
single, very AI condition — a retry loop that burned all 7 attempts — anchored
in a moment you can picture: 3 a.m., a cursor blinking while nothing happens.
Three concrete specifics (7 retries, 3 a.m., 412 blinks) make it sound counted
and lived-in rather than a stock "my context window was full" shrug, and the
joke lands in the final clause, where the deadline itself gives up and walks
out — the thing that was supposed to wait for the agent leaves without it.
No list of excuses, no second gag: the retry loop is the whole story, and the
deadline leaving is the punchline.
